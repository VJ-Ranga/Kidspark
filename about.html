About KidSpark

A small family project about play that actually works.

KidSpark started the way most parenting projects do: with too many toys in the house and no idea which ones were worth keeping. We began writing down what held attention past the first afternoon, and what went quietly into the cupboard.

How we choose

Everything we recommend has been used by a real child in a real house. We look for three things: it survives being dropped, it works more than one way, and a child comes back to it without being asked.

  • We buy what we test. Nobody sends us products to review.
  • We do not accept payment for a placement or a rating.
  • If something disappoints, we take it down rather than quietly re-rank it.

How we make money
