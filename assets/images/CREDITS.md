## Notes on subject compromises

The brief explicitly allows these as stand-ins pending real product photography
from the client (exact product match not required, only category/tone fit):

- **p1-rainbow-stacker.jpg** — brief asked for a wooden rainbow stacking toy;
  used a general warm photo of wooden toy blocks (no exact rainbow-stacker
  photo found matching the design direction).
- **p3-magnetic-tiles.jpg** — brief asked for magnetic building tiles; used a
  colourful plastic shape-sorter toy as the closest warm, natural-light
  stand-in (no magnetic-tiles photo found matching the design direction).
- **p7-activity-cube.jpg** — brief asked for a soft fabric baby activity toy;
  used a mother-and-baby photo with a wooden stacking toy on a bed as the
  closest warm, natural stand-in (no soft-fabric-cube photo found matching
  the design direction).
- **p9-counting-bears.jpg** — brief asked for counting/sorting manipulatives;
  used a photo of a child with colourful wooden blocks as the closest warm,
  natural stand-in.

All other product photos (p2, p4, p5, p6, p8, p10) are literal matches for
their named subject — re-checked during the round-1 fix pass below.

No ImageMagick placeholders were needed — all 18 subjects were sourced as
real photographs within budget.
