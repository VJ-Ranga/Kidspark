Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we do not.

This is a plain-language summary of how KidSpark at Home handles information. It applies to this website only.

What we collect

  • Newsletter signups. Your email address, and nothing else, if you choose to give it.
  • Messages you send us. The name, email and message you type into the contact form.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.
  • We do not ask children for information. This site is written for parents.

Links to other sites

Product links take you to merchants such as Amazon. Once you leave this site you are covered by their privacy policy, not ours.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in any email, or ask us to delete what we hold by writing to us on the contact page.
