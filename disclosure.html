Affiliate disclosure

How this site makes money, in plain terms.

KidSpark at Home takes part in affiliate programmes, including the Amazon Associates Programme. When you click a product link on this site and buy something, we may earn a small commission.

What this costs you

Nothing. The price you pay is the same whether you reach the merchant through us or go there directly.

What it does not change

  • What we recommend. We choose products before we check whether a programme covers them.
  • The order things appear in. Nobody can pay for a higher position.
  • What we say about them. If a product has a flaw, we write about the flaw.

How to spot an affiliate link

Every outbound product link on this site opens in a new tab and is marked with a green “View on…” button naming the merchant. There are no hidden affiliate links inside article text.

Prices

Prices shown are the ones we last saw at the merchant. They change often, and the merchant’s own page is always the accurate one.
